📜 HouseHub Property Listing Rules (नियम व शर्तें)

  • Rule 1 - Genuine Information: All property details, address, floor, and amenities provided must be 100% accurate and authentic.
  • Rule 2 - Transparent Pricing: The stated price / monthly rent must be genuine without hidden non-disclosed charges.
  • Rule 3 - Real Photos: Uploaded images must be recent and represent the actual condition of the property (no fake/misleading photos).
  • Rule 4 - Legal Authorization: You confirm that you are the direct owner or a licensed broker with authorization to list this property.
  • Rule 5 - Admin Verification: The listing will undergo Admin review before being made visible publicly to customers.
