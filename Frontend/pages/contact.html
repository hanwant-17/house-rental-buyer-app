Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about HouseHub

❓ How does broker verification work?

Every broker must submit legitimate government ID and address proofs during registration. Our Admin team audits these documents and issues an official Broker ID (e.g. BRK-2026-1001) before they can list properties or interact with clients.

❓ Why do properties need Admin verification?

To eliminate duplicate listings, fake images, and deceptive pricing, all property submissions undergo Admin review. Only properties meeting all verification criteria are published for buyers and tenants.

❓ How do I schedule a physical site visit?

Registered customers can visit any property details page and use the "Schedule Site Visit" tool to choose a preferred date and time slot. The broker confirms the appointment directly within the app.

❓ Is my personal contact information shared publicly?

No. HouseHub offers built-in direct In-App Chat. You can communicate with brokers and negotiate visits without your private mobile number being exposed.
