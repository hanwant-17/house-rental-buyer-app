FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

❓ How do I schedule a physical site visit for a property?

Simply navigate to any verified property in our Browse Listings page, click "View Details", and choose "Schedule Physical Visit" to select your preferred date and time slot with the broker.

❓ Are all property listings verified?

Yes. HouseHub enforces Rule 2: No property listing goes live on the marketplace without document checks and admin audit approval.

❓ How do I report a misleading listing or fake price?

You can click the "Report Property" button located on the details page. Our compliance admins review all reports and suspend fraudulent listings promptly.

❓ How does a broker get verified?

Brokers register with their government ID and address proofs. The admin team reviews the licensing documentation, assigns a unique Broker Code (e.g. BRK-2026-1001), and approves the account.
